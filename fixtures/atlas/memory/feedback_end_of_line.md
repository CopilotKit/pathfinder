After dispatching background agents, the availability signal is "End of line." — exactly three words, period included.

**Why:** User preference. Replaces the old "I'm here." signal from orchestrator-discipline.

**How to apply:** Any time you dispatch background agents and end your response with the availability signal, use "End of line." instead of "I'm here."
